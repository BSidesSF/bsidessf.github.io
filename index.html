BSidesSF 2026 is a wrap!

Thank you everyone!

In Case You Missed It

There was an amazing line-up of talks, workshops, birds-of-feather meetings, and meetups. We sifted through almost 600 submissions hoping you'll find interesting topics and inspiring talks in our final selections. You can see what you missed on the schedule

Most of our talks are posted on our YouTube channel. Find them on the
BSidesSF 2026 Playlist.

Thank You! BSidesSF 2026 Sponsors

This event would not have been possible without support from our sponsors. We sincerely thank them for being a part of our event and being willing to support us financially. Please take a moment to check them out.

Be sure to remind any sponsor you're talking with where you met them. This shows the marketing value BSidesSF brings, which encourages them to sponsor in the future.

Additionally, thank you to the Friends of BSidesSF who upgraded their tickets for the event t-shirt plus a special badge. We appreciate your generosity and desire to support BSidesSF.

BSidesSF 2027 will be April 3-4!

Work With Us!

Want to be a part of the team that makes this event happen year after year? It's a big responsibility and we'd welcome your aid. Please see our organizers interest page for more information and to apply.
